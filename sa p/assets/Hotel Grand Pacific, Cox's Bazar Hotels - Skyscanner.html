Comment: 9 Jul 2026

Stayed in: Jul 2026

2.0/5
Building works are ongoing.
Claims to have multiple swimming pools, I have only seen 1 in the 4 days I have been here.
Room is small, view is horrible and the balcony has no privacy as they are right next to each other.
Cleanliness is good as the hotel is new.
Bathroom is tiny, knee touches shower screen when sat down.
Shower pressure is low.
Bath towels are quite small.
Room service food was not great.
Buffet breakfast was mediocre at best, i understand that breakfast is included with the stay but the quality and taste does not reflect the price and the so called 5 star rating.
Staff- some were great and others certainly need training as how to talk, work in the hospitality sector, especially a so called 5 star hotel.
Not allowed to bring food or drink from outside hotel. Hotel only supply 500ml of water per day, extra charges apply for more.
Would I stay here again/ recommend?
